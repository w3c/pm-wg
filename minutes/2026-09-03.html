B) Update on the new ISO/IEC Adoption Process

I would like to give a brief update on the new ISO/IEC Adoption Process, which replaces the former PAS submission process.

First, organizations that were recognized PAS Submitters under the previous process are recognized submitters under the new Adoption Process. Therefore, W3C does not need to go through a new recognition process in order to use the Adoption Process.

Second, the new process involves two ballots. There is first a four-week ballot on whether to initiate the adoption process. If the process is initiated, the submitted document then proceeds to an enquiry ballot. Although this means that there are now two ballots, at present I do not expect this change by itself to make the overall process significantly longer.

There are still some details about the practical operation of the new process that I would like to clarify. I intend to raise the remaining questions at the JTC 1 Plenary in November. I hope that, after that meeting, I will be able to give W3C a clearer and more complete explanation of how the new Adoption Process will work in practice.

C) No Technical Corrigendum for EPUB Accessibility 1.0 at this time

Based on the recommendation from the Publishing Maintenance Working Group, I prepared draft text for a Technical Corrigendum to EPUB Accessibility 1.0.

However, ISO/CS advised SC 34 to use an Amendment rather than a Technical Corrigendum, because ISO/CS considers the proposed change to be beyond the permitted scope of a Technical Corrigendum.

An Amendment would require more time than a Technical Corrigendum. Moreover, once EPUB Accessibility 1.2 is adopted as an ISO/IEC International Standard, EPUB Accessibility 1.0 will be withdrawn from the ISO catalogue.

For these reasons, SC 34 has not taken further action on the proposed correction and is currently waiting for the submission of EPUB Accessibility 1.2.
